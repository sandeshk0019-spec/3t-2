1. ABOUT THE PROJECT (WHAT IS 3T?)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3T (Time To Time) is an enterprise-grade, offline-first, smart dairy collection 
and digital payout automation platform designed for rural India's dairy sector. 

In traditional dairy collection centers, milk procurement relies heavily on manual 
paper registers, delayed fat/SNF quality calculations, cash-based manual ledgers, 
and delayed payment disbursements (often taking weeks). This lack of transparency 
causes financial disputes, loss of farmer trust, rogue operator tampering, and zero 
audit trails.

3T solves this end-to-end through a digitized workflow:
  MILK INTAKE ➔ HARDWARE INGESTION ➔ QUALITY FORMULA ➔ INSTANT RECEIPT ➔ AUTOMATED PAYOUT

Core Metaphor: MILK ➔ DATA ➔ PRICE ➔ PAYMENT

Key Highlights:
- Sub-second quality pricing calculation.
- Instant digital payment generation via Razorpay / RazorpayX.
- Offline-first operational capability (local LowDB edge queue with auto-sync to cloud).
- Multilingual AI assistant ("AI Mitra") powered by Google Gemini API.
- Dual-Screen Customer Display broadcasting real-time data to farmers during milk pour.
- 2FA OTP authorization shielding sensitive farmer bank/UPI updates from fraud.

2. KEY FEATURES & INNOVATION PILLARS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[A] DIRECT HARDWARE INGESTION LAYER
- Web Serial API integration for RS232 / Serial Load Cell Weighing Scales and 
  Electronic Milk Testers (EMT / Ultrasonic Fat Analyzers).
- Auto-reads milk weight (kg), FAT percentage, and SNF (Solids-Not-Fat) percentage 
  without manual keyboard entry, preventing human operator error or fraud.

[B] DYNAMIC TWO-AXIS PRICING FORMULA ENGINE
- Centralized rule engine adhering to National Dairy Development Board (NDDB) 
  and FSSAI quality standards.
- Pricing Formula: Rate (INR/Liter) = Base Price + (FAT * f1) + (SNF * f2)
- Admins can dynamically adjust Base Price, FAT factor (f1), and SNF factor (f2) 
  across branches with real-time rate re-calculation.

[C] AUTOMATED PAYMENT & INSTANT PAYOUT ROUTING
- Automated settlement routing: Pending ➔ Processing ➔ Direct Bank/UPI Payout.
- Razorpay & RazorpayX Payout Gateway integration for instant direct transfers 
  to farmer bank accounts or UPI VPAs.
- Support for simulated payout mode when live gateway credentials are not attached.
- Automated payment ledger and digital passbook updates in <5 seconds.

[D] REAL-TIME MULTI-TIER VISIBILITY & DUAL-DISPLAY
- Farmer Live Mobile Passbook: Passwordless OTP login allowing farmers to view 
  daily milk pour receipts, FAT/SNF quality graphs, monthly earnings, and transaction status.
- Secondary Customer Display (Dual-Screen Sync): Real-time broadcast engine that displays 
  live weight, FAT, SNF, rate, and total payout on a second monitor facing the farmer.
- Multi-Branch Admin Dashboard: Centralized portal monitoring procurement volume, 
  total daily payouts, branch performance, and village-wise analytics.

[E] AI MITRA — MULTILINGUAL VOICE & TEXT ASSISTANT
- Integrated Google Gemini AI assistant ("AI Mitra") capable of speaking and 
  understanding English, Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, and other regional languages.
- Provides instant guidance on milk quality enhancement, cattle nutrition, rate chart 
  explanations, government dairy schemes, and app navigation.

[F] 2FA KYC SECURITY SHIELD & ZERO-KNOWLEDGE PII MASKING
- Rogue Operator Theft Prevention: 2FA Mobile OTP authorization sent to farmer's mobile 
  number whenever sensitive bank account numbers, IFSC codes, or UPI IDs are modified.
- Data Privacy Protection: Server-side PII masking for sensitive numbers (Aadhaar, Bank A/C).
- Soft-Delete & 15-Day Recovery Window: Soft-deleted farmer records are retained for 15 days 
  before permanent purge, preventing accidental data loss.

[G] SMS & BROADCAST ANNOUNCEMENT SYSTEM
- Integrated SMS dispatch engine sending instant SMS receipts after milk collection.
- Admin Broadcast Center enabling dairy management to send announcements, meeting alerts, 
  and bonus notifications to all registered farmers.

4. DATABASES USED (HYBRID / DUAL DATABASE ARCHITECTURE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3T uses a hybrid database model designed to guarantee offline continuity at village 
centers while maintaining cloud synchronization for multi-tier dairy operations:

1. LOCAL PERSISTENT EDGE DATABASE (LowDB):
   - Path: `data/threet_database.json` (or `/tmp/3t_data/threet_database.json` on Vercel)
   - Library: `lowdb` (JSON-backed local database)
   - Function: Acts as the primary zero-latency local store and offline buffer queue. 
     Ensures milk collection can continue seamlessly even during internet outages.

2. CLOUD DATABASE & ROW-LEVEL SECURITY (Supabase / PostgreSQL):
   - Service: Supabase (Managed PostgreSQL database)
   - SDK: `@supabase/supabase-js`
   - Features: Row-Level Security (RLS), realtime cloud backups, multi-branch data 
     aggregation, and automated edge-to-cloud synchronization.

3. MONGODB & MONGOOSE (Express Backend Database):
   - Service: MongoDB (via Mongoose ORM in `backend/server.js`)
   - Schemas: `Farmer`, `Collection`, `Payout`
   - Function: Handles secure payout queue, rate limiting, and webhook validation callbacks.

5. EXTERNAL APIS & INTEGRATIONS USED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. GOOGLE GEMINI AI API:
   - Endpoint: `https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com` (via `GEMINI_API_KEY`)
   - Module: `src/app/api/ai-mitra/route.ts` & `src/app/api/farmer/ask/route.ts`
   - Function: Powers the multilingual voice & text AI Mitra assistant.

2. RAZORPAY / RAZORPAYX PAYOUT API:
   - Endpoint: `https://api.razorpay.com/v1/payouts`
   - Keys: `NEXT_PUBLIC_RAZORPAY_KEY_ID`, `RAZORPAY_KEY_SECRET`, `PAYOUT_API_KEY`
   - Function: Triggers instant direct bank payouts (IMPS/NEFT/UPI) to farmers upon milk procurement.

3. RAZORPAY HMAC SHA-256 WEBHOOK DISPATCH:
   - Route: `/api/webhooks/payout` (Backend) & `/api/payments/razorpay` (Frontend)
   - Function: Validates payment status callbacks (`payout.processed`, `payout.failed`) 
     using cryptographic HMAC SHA-256 signatures (`x-razorpay-signature`).

4. WEB SERIAL API (Hardware Interface):
   - Browser Standard: `navigator.serial`
   - Function: Direct connection to RS232 Serial Weighing Scales and Electronic Milk Testers.

5. DLT / TWILIO SMS GATEWAY (SMS Notifications):
   - Module: `src/lib/notifications.ts`
   - Function: Sends real-time SMS receipts for milk intake and OTP verifications.

6. COMPLETE REST API ENDPOINT REFERENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

AUTHENTICATION & SECURITY APIs:
- POST `/api/auth/login`                   -> Authenticates Admin & Branch session cookies
- POST `/api/auth/logout`                  -> Clears active enterprise session cookie
- GET  `/api/auth/me`                      -> Returns active session role and authenticated status
- POST `/api/auth/farmer-otp/send`         -> Dispatches 6-digit login OTP to farmer's phone
- POST `/api/auth/farmer-otp/verify`       -> Verifies farmer login OTP and sets session
- POST `/api/auth/farmer-otp/logout`       -> Destroys farmer login session
- POST `/api/auth/bank-update-otp/send`    -> 2FA Shield: Sends OTP for updating sensitive bank details
- POST `/api/auth/bank-update-otp/verify`  -> 2FA Shield: Verifies OTP before saving bank changes

FARMER MANAGEMENT APIs:
- GET    `/api/farmers`                    -> Lists all registered farmers (with cloud merge)
- POST   `/api/farmers`                    -> Registers a new farmer record
- PUT    `/api/farmers`                    -> Updates farmer demographics or bank info (2FA protected)
- DELETE `/api/farmers`                    -> Soft-deletes a farmer record (15-day recovery window)
- GET    `/api/farmer/[farmerId]/data`     -> Serves personal passbook history and earnings data
- GET/POST `/api/farmer/notifications`     -> Retrieves or posts targeted farmer notifications

MILK COLLECTION & PRICING APIs:
- GET  `/api/collections`                  -> Fetches milk intake records across collection centers
- POST `/api/collections`                  -> Submits new milk pour entry, auto-computes price formula, 
                                              updates ledger, and triggers instant payout pipeline

PAYMENTS & PAYOUT APIs:
- GET  `/api/payments`                     -> Lists payment transactions and settlement statuses
- POST `/api/payments`                     -> Manually triggers or retries farmer payment payout
- POST `/api/payments/razorpay`            -> Directly calls RazorpayX gateway for instant payout

SYSTEM, AI & UTILITY APIs:
- POST `/api/ai-mitra`                     -> Multilingual AI Assistant query processing (Gemini)
- POST `/api/farmer/ask`                   -> Farmer voice/text assistant interface
- GET/POST `/api/settings`                 -> Reads or modifies Base Price, FAT factor, SNF factor
- GET/POST `/api/display-sync`             -> Realtime WebSocket / polling sync for Dual-Screen Customer Display
- POST `/api/admin/broadcast`              -> Sends broadcast announcements to farmer mobile apps
- GET  `/api/branches`                     -> Returns multi-branch procurement performance data
- GET  `/api/manifest`                     -> Generates dynamic PWA web manifest JSON
- GET  `/api/seed-supabase`                -> Seeds initial dairy test records into cloud Supabase

EXPRESS BACKEND APIs (Port 5000):
- GET  `/api/farmers`                      -> Retrieves farmers from MongoDB
- GET  `/api/collections`                  -> Retrieves collections from MongoDB
- GET  `/api/payments`                     -> Retrieves payouts from MongoDB
- POST `/api/pay-farmer`                   -> Validates input (Joi), checks rate-limit, calls RazorpayX
- POST `/api/webhooks/payout`              -> Validates HMAC signature, updates transaction status in DB

7. COMPLETE STEP-BY-STEP PROJECT WORKFLOW SCRIPT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[SCENE 1: MILK INTAKE AT THE VILLAGE COLLECTION CENTER]
1. The farmer arrives at the collection center with raw milk.
2. Operator opens the 3T Collection Terminal on the browser.
3. Operator scans the farmer's QR code card or types their ID / Mobile number.
4. Scale auto-captures the weight (e.g. 15.4 kg) via Web Serial API.
5. Electronic Milk Tester (EMT) auto-captures FAT (e.g. 4.5%) and SNF (e.g. 8.8%).

[SCENE 2: AUTOMATED PRICING & DUAL-SCREEN BROADCAST]
6. 3T Pricing Engine instantly executes the formula:
   Rate = Base Price (₹40.00) + (4.5 * ₹1.50) + (8.8 * ₹1.00) = ₹55.55 / kg
   Total Payout = 15.4 kg * ₹55.55 = ₹855.47
7. The Dual-Screen Customer Display update fires via `/api/display-sync`. The farmer 
   sees their weight, fat, SNF, rate, and exact total on the secondary customer screen.

[SCENE 3: DIGITAL LEDGER, INSTANT SMS & AUTOMATED PAYOUT]
8. The collection entry is stored in local LowDB (`data/threet_database.json`) and 
   upserted to Cloud Supabase (`collections` and `payments` tables).
9. An instant SMS receipt is dispatched to the farmer's mobile phone.
10. The payment pipeline status transitions: `Pending` ➔ `Processing` ➔ `Success`.
11. RazorpayX Payout API transfers ₹855.47 directly to the farmer's bank account or UPI ID.

[SCENE 4: FARMER MOBILE PASSBOOK & AI MITRA HELP]
12. The farmer logs into the 3T Mobile Passbook using 6-Digit Mobile OTP (`/api/auth/farmer-otp/send`).
13. Farmer views their digital passbook, milk pour history, and instant bank credit notification.
14. If the farmer has questions about milk rate or cattle feed, they click "AI Mitra" 
    and ask in Marathi/Hindi. Gemini AI responds instantly with clear audio/text advice.

[SCENE 5: DAIRY ADMIN OVERVIEW & SECURITY]
15. Dairy Admin opens the Admin Dashboard (`/dashboard`) to monitor procurement totals, 
    view multi-branch performance analytics, update rate formulas, or send broadcast SMS.
16. If any operator attempts to alter a farmer's bank details, the 2FA KYC Shield triggers, 
    requiring an OTP sent directly to the farmer's mobile before any change is saved.
