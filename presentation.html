Key Risk Analysis & Architectural Mitigations

• Rogue Operator Theft: 2FA Farmer Mobile OTP required for Bank/UPI edits + Duplicate Lock
• Intermittent Internet: Local edge buffer queue; automatically syncs to cloud on reconnection
• Reading Disputes: Quality threshold validation + immutable digital receipt sent directly to farmer
• Data Privacy: Server-side Zero-Knowledge masking (Aadhaar: XXXX-XXXX-1234, Bank: ••••••••5678)
