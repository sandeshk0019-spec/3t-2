Act 1: The Hook & The Problem (0:00 – 0:45)

⏱️ 0:00 – 0:45 Screen on: https://3tdairy.vercel.app
🗣️ WHAT TO SAY (Speaker):

"Respected judges, imagine a dairy farmer in rural India who wakes up at 4:30 AM, milks his cows, and delivers 15 liters of milk to the collection booth twice a day. He needs cash today to buy cattle feed and groceries. But under India’s current dairy system, he has to wait 10 to 15 days for a batch bank transfer. In the meantime, he is forced to borrow from local moneylenders at 36% annual interest.

Worse, corrupt booth operators can silently change his bank account number in desktop software to steal his money.

We present 3T — Time To Time Dairy Platform. The UPI moment for Indian Dairy. Milk Now, Money in Bank in 3 Seconds with Zero-Trust Security. Let us show you our working prototype live."

⏱️ 4:00 – 4:45 Screen on: /dashboard (Edit Farmer Profile Modal)

Judge Q&A Rapid-Fire Cheat Sheet (Top 5 Comebacks)

Q1: "Gokul/Amul already calculate FAT/SNF. What's new?"

Say: "Calculation is basic math; our innovation is what happens 1 millisecond later: instant sub-3-second bank payout directly over NPCI rails, zero-trust 2FA OTP operator protection, and Marathi Voice AI."

Q2: "What if internet goes down in the village?"

Say: "Our PWA operates 100% offline via IndexedDB. The hardware logs milk entries locally with signed vouchers. The instant 4G reconnects, it syncs and executes payouts automatically."

Q3: "Who pays the transaction fee?"

Say: "UPI payouts operate with Zero MDR under NPCI guidelines. IMPS costs ~₹1.50, which the dairy gladly absorbs because it saves over ₹15 per farmer on manual cash handling and branch reconciliation."

Q4: "What if milk is adulterated after payment?"

Say: "Quality analysis (FAT, SNF, CLR, Water %) is verified by hardware BEFORE the milk is poured into the common collection vat. Payment triggers ONLY after sensor validation passes."
