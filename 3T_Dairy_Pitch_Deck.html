THE GROUND REALITY

3 Pain Points Holding Back 80 Million Indian Farmers

Why traditional manual registers and delayed cooperative cycles hurt rural livelihood

⏳

15–20 Day Payment Delay

Farmers work 365 days a year starting before sunrise and incur daily expenses for cattle feed and medicine. Yet, payments are held for weeks, forcing them into high-interest local debt.

🚨 Result: Severe rural working-capital crisis
❌

Opacity & Manual Tampering

Milk weight, FAT%, and SNF% are written by hand in paper registers. Centers frequently under-read metrics, reducing farmer income by 10%–18% with zero proof for dispute resolution.

🚨 Result: Chronic distrust between farmers & dairies
📉

Zero Early Disease Alerts

When cattle fall ill (e.g., subclinical mastitis), milk yield drops silently. Farmers realize too late, causing permanent damage to livestock health and ₹4,000+ loss per cow.

🚨 Result: High cattle mortality & production loss

TECHNICAL EXCELLENCE

Scalable, Enterprise & Offline-First Architecture

Built to withstand harsh rural internet drops with zero data loss

💻 Frontend & Presentation Layer

  • • Next.js 16 (App Router) & React 19: Blazing fast SSR + CSR.
  • • Tailwind CSS & Framer Motion: High-DPI responsive UI.
  • • Web Bluetooth API (BLE 4.0+): Direct hardware scale connectivity.
  • • BroadcastChannel API: Sub-millisecond dual display sync.

⚙️ Business Logic & Intelligence

  • • Dynamic Rate Engine: Configurable Base + FAT + SNF formula.
  • • Milk Drop Detection: Rolling 7-day anomaly detection engine.
  • • Zero-Knowledge Security: Server-side PII masking for Aadhaar/Bank.
  • • OTP Shield: 6-digit OTP verification for bank modifications.

💾 Hybrid Database & Edge Sync

  • • Write-Through Storage: Immediate disk serialization.
  • • Supabase Cloud PostgreSQL: State-wide multi-branch sync.
  • • Local LowDB Cache: Zero-downtime offline rural collection.
  • • Atomic Write Lock: Eliminates concurrent overwrite races.

🏦 Banking Rails & AI Engine

  • • NPCI UPI / IMPS Gateway: Automated 2-second bank transfer.
  • • RazorpayX Integration: Live sandbox & multi-account payouts.
